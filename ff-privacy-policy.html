FileShare Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

FileShare transfers files directly between your Android phone and your computer over local Wi-Fi.

1. Information and file access

FileShare accesses files you choose to send or receive. Files move between your connected devices, not through a developer-operated server. According to the current app implementation, FileShare does not send your transferred files or local transfer history to the developer or advertising services. Google’s advertising and consent SDKs process information as described in section 5.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Preferences: device name, connection approval and timeout settings, save-folder choice, and other app preferences.
  • Optional transfer history: local records, which may include file names, sizes, transfer direction, status, time, speed, and save location. You can disable history or clear it in Settings → Privacy & data → Clear transfer history.
  • Temporary shared-file copies: files you select to share may be staged in the app's cache. These copies are removed when you remove them from sharing, stop sharing, or uninstall the app.
  • Received files: stored in Downloads/FileShare by default on supported Android versions, or in a folder you choose.
  • Pairing information: pairing codes and active connection sessions are kept temporarily on the phone and expire or end when sharing stops.

3. Local network and security

FileShare runs a local server while sharing is active. Devices must pair before accessing shared files; phone approval is enabled by default. You can disconnect a computer or stop sharing.

Local file transfers use HTTP and are not encrypted. Someone able to intercept traffic on your network could read transferred information. Use FileShare only on trusted, private networks. The Android INTERNET permission is used for local network communication; it does not mean that file transfers use cloud servers.

4. Data retention and deletion

You can clear transfer history in the app. Stopping sharing removes staged shared-file copies and ends active sessions. Uninstalling the app removes its private settings, history, and cache. Files saved to a public Downloads folder or another folder you selected may remain after uninstalling; delete those files using your file manager. Files already transferred to a computer must be managed on that computer.

FileShare does not maintain a developer-operated account or file database, so there is no developer-held account or file archive to delete.

5. Third-party services and advertising

FileShare uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information, including device identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, to deliver, measure, and improve advertising and help prevent fraud.

Where required, FileShare uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request advertising consent and provide privacy choices. Users can review or update their advertising preferences through the privacy options available in the app, where applicable.

FileShare does not upload your transferred files to Google AdMob. File transfers take place directly between your devices over the local Wi-Fi network.

For more information about Google's data practices, please read the Google Privacy Policy .

Opening this privacy policy may connect your browser to the website hosting it. That website may process standard connection information under its own privacy practices.

6. Children's privacy

FileShare is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to FileShare. The latest version and update date will be published here.
