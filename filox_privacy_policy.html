Privacy Policy

Your documents are processed on your device. Filox does not operate an account system or an app-owned server for uploading your documents. The app does use Google services for advertising, consent management, barcode recognition, text recognition, diagnostics, and service security. Those services process the limited technical data described below.

1. Scope and developer

This policy applies to the Filox Android application, package name com.filox.app, developed by Maicoders. It explains what data the app accesses, what remains on your device, what is sent to service providers, why it is used, and the controls available to you.

Filox does not require an account, name, phone number, address, or login. Filox does not sell your documents or document contents.

2. Files, images, camera, and permissions

Files you choose

When you select a PDF, document, image, archive, or other file through Android's system picker, Filox receives permission to read that item and, when required for a requested edit, write the resulting file. File content is processed locally on your device.

All files access

On supported Android versions, Filox may ask you to enable the special "All files access" setting. This access is used only for user-facing file-management features that require a device-wide view, including storage analysis, finding duplicate files, finding large files, and managing files selected from those results. Without this access, you can still use tools that work with files you select through Android's system picker.

Filox does not upload files discovered by a storage scan. A scan may read file names, paths, sizes, modification dates, and file bytes when needed to compare content or perform the action you request.

Camera

Camera access is used for document capture and QR or barcode scanning. Camera frames and captured images are processed locally. Filox does not request microphone access and does not record audio.

Saved output

Files you save are written to /Downloads/Filox or another location you choose through Android. These output files remain on your device until you delete them. If you use Android's share action, the file is sent only to the app or person you choose.

3. Data stored locally

Recent activityFile URI, file name, displayed size, tool used, status, and timestamp. The list is limited to 100 entries.
PreferencesTheme, onboarding state, confirmation settings, favorites, and the expiry time of an ad-supported Pro pass.
Temporary filesWorking copies, previews, page renders, and generated output waiting to be saved or shared.
Permission recordsAndroid may retain access grants for files you selected so Filox can reopen them from recent activity.

Android cloud backup is disabled for Filox. Locally stored history, preferences, and temporary work are not intentionally uploaded by Filox for backup.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and recent activity remain until you remove them, use "Clear All Data" in Filox Settings, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Temporary files may also be removed by Android when storage is needed. Files saved in /Downloads/Filox are not deleted by "Clear All Data" or by uninstalling Filox; you control those files through your device's file manager.

4. Google services and data sent off-device

Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform

Filox uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and opt-in rewarded ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform to collect and manage consent choices where required. According to Google's disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK, it may automatically collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app interactions such as launches, taps, and ad video views;
  • diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage; and
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, consent management, and service operation. Data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's policies.

Learn more in Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google ML Kit

Filox uses bundled Google ML Kit features for on-device text recognition and barcode scanning. Your image and recognized document content are not sent to Filox servers. Google states that ML Kit SDKs may collect device information, app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, feature input/output size, feature versions, event types, and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this technical data is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties.

Learn more in Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

6. Security

Filox reduces exposure by processing document content locally, using Android system pickers for ordinary file selection, requesting special storage access only for device-wide file-management tools, disabling app backup, and relying on Android's app sandbox. Google states that the SDK data described above is encrypted in transit. No application or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Filox is a general productivity application and is not directed to children under 13. Maicoders does not knowingly request names, contact details, or accounts from children. The app caps AdMob content at a PG rating. If you believe a child has provided information through a support request, contact us so it can be addressed.

8. Your controls

  • File access: choose individual files through Android's picker, revoke persisted access through "Clear All Data," or disable "All files access" in Android Settings.
  • Camera: deny or revoke camera permission in Android Settings. File import remains available.
  • Advertising consent: open Filox Settings and select "Privacy & Consent Choices" where the option is available.
  • Advertising identifier: reset or delete the advertising ID using your Android privacy settings.
  • Local records: clear recent history, clear temporary files, or use "Clear All Data" in Filox Settings.
