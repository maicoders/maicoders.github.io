Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026
Filox does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
All file processing happens entirely on your device. Your files never leave your phone.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Filox does not require an account, login, or registration of any kind.

2. File Access

Filox requests access to your device storage solely to read and process files you explicitly select. No file content is uploaded, transmitted, or stored outside your device.

3. Camera & Microphone

Filox requests camera permission for the Document Scanner and QR & Barcode Studio and Image features. Camera input is processed locally in real time and is never recorded, stored remotely, or shared.

4. Advertising (AdMob)

Filox displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy. You can review it at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Analytics

Filox does not use any analytics SDK or crash reporting service that transmits data to external servers.

6. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into Filox is Google AdMob. No other external service, API, or backend is used.

7. Children's Privacy

Filox is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of the app after any change constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
