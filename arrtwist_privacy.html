Privacy Policy

Arrow Twist · Effective 14 November 2026

This policy explains how Arrow Twist (package com.arrowstwist.puzzle), published by MAICoder's Inc, handles information.

Information we handle

Arrow Twist does not require an account and does not ask for a name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment details. Game progress, stars, settings, streaks, and earned Hint and Undo actions are stored locally on the device and are not sent to our servers.

Advertising and third-party services

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google User Messaging Platform, and Google Play services. Google may automatically process and share an IP address (which may indicate general location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic or performance information, and device or app identifiers for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. Arrow Twist disables access to the Android Advertising ID and configures ads for child-directed treatment with a G content rating.

Where required, the game asks for a privacy choice before requesting ads and provides an Ad privacy choices entry in Settings. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google's partner data explanation. We do not sell personal information.

Children

Arrow Twist is designed to be child-friendly. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. Advertising requests use child-directed and under-age-of-consent settings.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until it is deleted. It can be erased at any time through Settings → Privacy & Support → Delete local game data, or by uninstalling the game. Ad privacy choices are managed separately through the in-game Ad privacy choices entry when available. Information processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and user controls.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The new effective date will appear above. For privacy questions, contact Email.
