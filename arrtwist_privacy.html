Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Arrows twist is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains, briefly, what data is involved.

No accounts, no personal info

Arrows twist does not require sign-up or login, and does not collect your name, email, or any personal information.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect your advertising ID, IP address, and basic device/diagnostic data to serve and measure ads. This data is encrypted in transit and handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Your controls

You can wipe all local app data anytime from Settings → Reset/Clear Data inside the app. You can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID anytime in your device's system settings, or remove all app data by uninstalling the app.

Children

Arrows twist is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page.
